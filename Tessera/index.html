TESSERA ∞

An endless field of rectangular blocks. Slide, smash, purify.

The grid

The world is an infinite grid of rectangles of random size, each printed with its own pattern. Blocks never push each other and never pass through one another. No diagonal moves — only along rows and columns. A permanent hole sits at the world's origin, and more holes are scattered everywhere.

Holes

Slide a block onto a hole and the block is absorbed — points per cell: 10 normal, 15 blue, 20 gray, 25 golden (snapped-off red). Holes are hungry: a normal hole (including the one at the origin) must swallow 4 pieces before it seals, and a hole opened by a purified pattern swallows 20 pieces. The pips / tick ring around a hole show how many absorptions it has left.

Sectors

The world is split into sectors of 16×16 cells. Only the central one starts unlocked; blocks cannot enter, leave or move inside a locked sector. Click the button on a locked sector next to an unlocked one to buy it with points — each purchase makes the next one more expensive. The cells cleared counter adds up every cell left empty when a piece is destroyed (absorbed, purified or shattered).

Tainted patterns

Patterns of tainted cells hide underneath blocks. Slide a block away to discover them (purple dashed outline). Cover the tainted shape exactly with whole blocks — no overhang, nothing left open — and the covering blocks are destroyed, the taint is cleansed, you score big, and a brand new hole opens at the pattern's center.

Block types
  • Normal — drag anywhere, slides one cell at a time along the drag axis.
  • Gray (rail) — locked to a single axis, marked by the red double arrow inside.
  • Blue (slider) — moves in any direction but keeps going until it crashes into something.
  • Red (locked) — cannot be moved at all.
  • Orange — red cells that snapped off after a blue crash. Normal & movable.
Blue × Red crash

When a sliding blue block hits a red block, the blue cells in contact shatter (splitting the blue block), while the red cells in contact detach into new normal blocks of their own.

Controls: drag a block → move it · drag empty space / right-drag / space+drag → pan · wheel or pinch → zoom · click a block then arrow keys → step it · WASD → pan · Esc → deselect.
Touch: one finger → move blocks & tap buttons · two fingers → pan & zoom.
